# title: Obu Demo Move and Pick
# description: Navigate using movetopse to certain position in front of a table from robot home base, then pick the object.
# safety: supervise
# fresh_context: true

Zero odometry.
Park your arm at the zero pose.
Use MoveToPose to go to the odometry-frame pose X=1.076 m, Y=0.234 m, heading 86.5°.
Use MoveToPose to go to the odometry-frame pose X=1.076 m, Y=0.817 m, heading 86.5°.

Raise your arm to the ready pose.
Where is the banana? Use the wrist camera.
Now grasp the banana by calling the PickAt skill with the exact x, y, z coordinates you found in the previous step (pick at that known position — do not re-detect or just take a photo).
Hand it over to the person in front of you using the rear camera.
Park your arm at the rest pose and confirm the task is complete.
