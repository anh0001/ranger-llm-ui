# title: Pick & Deliver Demo
# description: Locate an object, grasp it at that 3D position with PickAt, then hand it to a person. Needs the arm + manipulation stack.
# safety: supervise
# fresh_context: true

Raise your arm to the ready pose.
Where is the banana? Use the wrist camera.
Now grasp the banana by calling the PickAt skill with the exact x, y, z coordinates you found in the previous step (pick at that known position — do not re-detect or just take a photo).
Hand it over to the person in front of you using the rear camera.
Park your arm at the rest pose and confirm the task is complete.
